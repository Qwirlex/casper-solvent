# Solvent, 3 minute video script (simple English)

Spoken voice over. Plain words. About 430 words, that is roughly 3 minutes at a calm
pace. Record the screen as you read each part. Pause between sections.

---

## 0:00 to 0:20, the hook

(Show the landing page at caspersolvent.xyz)

"This is Solvent. It is a savings vault on the Casper blockchain, and it is run by an AI
agent. You put money in, the agent grows the pool, and you take out your share plus what
it earned. Let me show you how it works."

## 0:20 to 0:45, the idea in plain words

(Slowly scroll the page)

"Think of it like a smart savings account. You are not the one watching the market. An
AI agent does that for you. It works on its own, day and night. And here is the key part.
The agent cannot run away with your money. The rules are written into the contract on the
blockchain, so it can only do what it is allowed to do."

## 0:45 to 1:30, what the agent does

(Show the What the agent did panel with the activity cards)

"Every few minutes the agent does a full cycle. First it buys fresh market data and a
risk score from two other agents. It pays them with a tiny on chain payment, using a
system called x402. This is machine to machine money, one agent paying another, with no
human in the middle.

Then it decides. If the risk looks low, it puts more of the pool into growth. If the
risk looks high, it plays it safe. Finally it adds yield to the pool and writes the whole
decision on the blockchain, where anyone can check it."

## 1:30 to 2:10, your money grows

(Show the vault card, assets per share, and the position panel)

"Here is the pool. You can see the value per share going up over time. That is real
yield, paid into the vault on chain.

Now watch a deposit. I connect my wallet. I approve the vault, then I deposit. I get
shares that represent my slice of the pool. As the agent adds yield, my shares are worth
more. When I withdraw, I get back what I put in plus my profit. Real tokens, real
transactions, all on the Casper test network."

## 2:10 to 2:40, why Casper

(Show the on chain proof cards or an explorer page)

"Why build this on Casper? Because Casper is the first blockchain with built in machine
payments. That is what lets one agent pay another for data and risk, automatically. An
agent that earns and spends on its own is a real economic actor. This only works on a
chain made for it."

## 2:40 to 3:00, close

(Back to the landing page, show the links)

"Solvent. An AI agent that runs a savings vault and pays its own way. It is live on the
Casper test network, the code is open source, and you can try it right now. Thanks for
watching."

(On screen: caspersolvent.xyz and github.com/Qwirlex/casper-solvent)

---

## Tips
- Read slowly, leave small pauses, do not rush.
- If a live action is slow on camera, cut to it already done.
- Keep the tone calm and plain, let the screen do the work.
